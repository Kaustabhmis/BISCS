How to Rearrange PDF Pages with Drag and Drop (Free)

Reorder, rotate, duplicate and delete PDF pages visually, insert blank pages and add pages from other PDFs. Free, browser-based and private. Full how-to guide.

By BISCS India Editorial Team Updated 2 November 2026 5 min read
Quick answer

To rearrange PDF pages, open a page organiser, drag the page thumbnails into the order you want, rotate or delete any page, then save the new PDF. BISCS India’s Organize PDF also inserts blank pages, duplicates pages and adds pages from other PDFs, all in your browser without uploading.

Organize PDF

Open Organize PDF

To rearrange pages in a PDF, open Organize PDF, drag the page thumbnails into the order you want, and save. While you are there, you can rotate sideways pages, delete unwanted ones, duplicate a page, insert blank pages and even pull in pages from another PDF.

It is the "do everything with pages" tool. And like the other BISCS PDF tools, it runs in your browser, so the document is not uploaded anywhere.

What you can do in one screen

Hover over a page (or tap it on a phone) to reveal its actions:

  • Move: drag the handle to a new position.
  • Rotate left or right: fix pages that were scanned sideways.
  • Duplicate: make a copy of a page, for example a form you need twice.
  • Insert blank page after: add an empty page at that point.
  • Delete: remove the page from the result.

Above the grid, Add PDF appends the pages of other files so you can mix them in. That turns the organiser into a flexible merge tool with page-level control.

How to organise PDF pages step by step

  1. Open Organize PDF and choose your PDF.
  2. Wait for the thumbnails. Each page shows its original page number.
  3. Drag pages into the order you want. The grid reflows as you move them.
  4. Hover or tap a page to rotate, duplicate, insert a blank page after it, or delete it.
  5. Click Add PDF if you need pages from another document, then position them.
  6. Look through the grid once more from start to finish.
  7. Save and download the new PDF.

When page order really matters

Scanned in the wrong order. You fed a stack of papers into a scanner upside down, and the PDF runs from the last page to the first. Drag them into place, or rebuild the order page by page.

Double-sided scans done in two passes. Scanning all the fronts, then all the backs, gives pages 1, 3, 5, then 2, 4, 6. The organiser lets you interleave them by dragging.

Application bundles. Portals and reviewers often expect a specific sequence: application form, photo ID, address proof, supporting documents. Put your bundle in the order listed in the instructions.

Reports and project files. Moving an executive summary to the front, or appendices to the back, is a common last-minute request in offices.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Losing track in long documents. For files over 50 pages, work in sections: first fix the order of the beginning, then scroll on. Each thumbnail shows its original number, which helps you spot pages that are out of place.
  • Deleting instead of moving. If a page disappears unexpectedly, check whether you clicked delete rather than the move handle. Starting again from the original file is quick if needed.
  • Forgetting about double-sided printing. If the PDF will be printed on both sides, insert a blank page wherever a new section must start on the front of a sheet.
  • Rotating the wrong way. A page rotated left when it needed right ends up upside down. Look at the thumbnail after each click.

Example: preparing a scholarship application

Ananya is applying for a state scholarship. The instructions ask for one PDF in this order: application form, income certificate, caste certificate, Class 12 mark sheet, bank passbook front page. She has a scanned PDF of the form and certificates in a random order, and the mark sheet and passbook as separate PDFs from DigiLocker and her bank.

She opens the main scan in the organiser, adds the other two files with Add PDF, drags each page into the listed order, rotates the sideways income certificate, and deletes a blank page the scanner added. The final file is in the exact order the reviewer expects. If the result is too large for the portal's limit (which, like every portal, has its own rules you should check), she can run it through Compress PDF next.

Interleaving a two-pass double-sided scan

This is one of the most common jobs for the organiser, so it is worth a short walkthrough. Say you scanned a 5-sheet, double-sided letter on a scanner that only scans one side at a time. You fed the fronts first, then flipped the stack and fed the backs. The PDF now reads: front 1, front 2, front 3, front 4, front 5, back 1, back 2, back 3, back 4, back 5.

Drag "back 1" (PDF page 6) to sit after page 1, "back 2" (page 7) after page 2, and so on. The original page numbers under each thumbnail make it easy to keep track. If you flipped the stack the other way, the backs may also be in reverse order (back 5 first), so look at the content of each page rather than trusting the sequence. When you are done, the document reads front and back in the right order, just like the paper original.

How the organiser handles different page sizes

Pages keep their own size and orientation when you move them. You can place an A4 letter next to a smaller receipt page or a landscape spreadsheet, and each stays as it was. Blank pages you insert take the size of the page they follow, so they fit naturally into the document when printed.

Tips for neat results

  • Insert a blank page between sections of a printed handout to make stapling and sorting easier.
  • Duplicate a page when you need an extra copy of a declaration or a cover sheet.
  • Rename the saved file to describe its contents. "Scholarship-application-Ananya-Das.pdf" is better than "document-organized.pdf".
  • If thumbnails are hard to read on a small phone screen, zoom the browser or switch to a larger device for long documents.

Privacy

Rearranging documents often means handling exactly the papers you would least like to leak: certificates, ID proofs, financial records. Here, everything is done by code running in your own browser tab. The file is read from your device and the new one is written back to it, with no copy left on any server.

Frequently asked questions

Can I combine pages from different PDFs in one go?

Yes. Use the Add PDF button to bring in the pages of another file. They appear in the same grid, and you can drag them in between the pages of the first document, rotate them or delete them before saving everything as one PDF.

Why would I insert a blank page?

Blank pages are useful for double-sided printing, so that each chapter or form starts on the front of a sheet. They also act as separators in a bundle of documents, or give you space to add handwritten notes, stamps or remarks after printing.

Does rotating a page here change its quality?

No. Rotation changes the page’s orientation setting rather than re-drawing it, so text and images stay exactly as sharp as before and the text remains selectable. The same applies to moving and duplicating pages.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. On touch screens, tap a page to show its action buttons for rotate, duplicate, blank page and delete, and drag the move handle to reposition it. Larger documents are easier to arrange on a tablet or computer screen.

What happens to the original file?

Nothing. The tool builds a new PDF from your arrangement and downloads it, while the original file on your device stays exactly as it was. Keep it until you have checked the new version.
