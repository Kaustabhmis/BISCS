How to split a photo into an Instagram grid

  1. Open the Instagram Grid & Story Maker and choose the Instagram grid splitter mode.
  2. Choose a photo, or paste one with Ctrl+V.
  3. Pick the Rows (3 photos each): 3 × 1, 3 × 2, 3 × 3 or 3 × 4.
  4. Choose the Tile size: 1080 × 1350 (4:5) or 1080 × 1080 (1:1). Instagram's profile grid now shows taller, portrait-shaped thumbnails, so 4:5 tiles generally sit better than squares; use the preview to check.
  5. Drag the photo to choose the crop, scroll or use Zoom to scale it, or press Centre photo.
  6. Check the Profile preview to see how the tiles will look together on your profile.
  7. Press Download ZIP. Each tile is a separate JPG, named in posting order.
  8. Follow the How to post note: upload tile 01 first (the bottom-right piece) and finish with the top-left tile.

That order matters because Instagram always places your newest post at the top left. Posting in the wrong order scrambles the picture.

How to design a post or story

  1. Switch to the Post & story designer.
  2. Pick a preset size, such as Instagram story or LinkedIn post.
  3. Start from a template: sale announcement, quote, event or webinar, "We are hiring", festival greeting, product launch, or milestone and thank-you.
  4. Set the background: a solid colour, a gradient or your own photo, with blur and darkening so text stays readable.
  5. Edit the layers. Add text, stickers (icons), shapes or photo layers; change fonts, weights, colours and alignment; duplicate, reorder or delete layers.
  6. For tall formats, keep Show story safe zones on and keep text out of the red bands.
  7. Choose PNG or JPG and press Download.

Ideas that work well for Indian businesses

  • Festival greetings. The festival template is a quick start for Diwali, Eid, Durga Puja, Pongal or Christmas posts. Swap in your brand colours and logo for a consistent look.
  • Sale and offer posts. "Flat 30% off this weekend" with a clear end date and your shop's location or WhatsApp number.
  • Hiring posts. A "We are hiring" graphic for LinkedIn with the role, city and how to apply.
  • Webinars and workshops. Date, time in IST, speaker name and a short registration link.
  • Milestones. "Thank you for 1,000 customers" or a five-year anniversary feels personal and earns engagement.

For a WhatsApp Business status, use the vertical preset and keep the message to a few words; people tap through statuses quickly.

Common mistakes with grids and stories

  • Posting tiles out of order. Follow the numbers. If you post something else in the middle, the grid breaks.
  • Faces on tile edges. A face split between two tiles looks odd in the feed, where each tile is seen alone. Shift the crop so key details sit inside one tile.
  • Text in story safe zones. Profile names, buttons and reply bars cover the top and bottom.
  • Tiny text. Most people view on phones. If you cannot read it at arm's length, make it bigger.
  • Too many fonts. Two fonts, at most, look more professional than five.
  • Heavy exports. If a platform complains about file size, compress the image with the Image Compressor.

Grid post, carousel or single image: which should you use?

Each format does a different job, so choose based on where people will see it.

A grid makes a strong first impression on your profile page. It suits launches, rebrands and portfolio showcases, for example an architect revealing a finished project across nine tiles. The trade-off is that each tile appears alone in followers' feeds, so every piece should still look intentional on its own.

A carousel keeps several images together in one post that people swipe through. It is better for step-by-step tips, before-and-after comparisons and product details, because the story stays in one place.

A single designed post is best for announcements with one clear message, such as an offer, an event date or a festival greeting. Most day-to-day posting falls into this category.

Tips for a cohesive feed

Pick two or three brand colours and use them in every design. If you do not have a logo yet, the Logo Maker creates a simple one you can drop into posts as a photo layer. For multi-photo posts such as event recaps, the Collage Maker arranges several pictures in one image.

Plan grids in batches. A 3 × 3 grid needs nine posts in a row; schedule them together so your profile is not half-finished for days.

Frequently asked questions

In what order should I post Instagram grid tiles?

Instagram shows your newest post at the top left, so post the bottom-right tile first and the top-left tile last. The BISCS tool numbers each tile and names the files in posting order, starting with 01, so you simply upload them one by one in sequence.

What size should an Instagram post be?

Commonly used sizes are 1080 × 1080 for square posts, 1080 × 1350 for portrait 4:5 posts and 1080 × 1920 for stories and reels. Instagram’s profile grid now shows taller thumbnails than the old squares, so portrait posts usually fit better. Check Instagram’s help pages, as display rules change.

What are story safe zones?

On stories and reels, the top of the screen shows your profile name and progress bar, and the bottom shows reply and action buttons. Text placed there can be hidden. The designer shows red bands for these areas, which are not exported, so you can keep important content clear.

Can I use these designs for WhatsApp status?

Yes. Choose the WhatsApp status preset, which uses a 1080 × 1920 vertical canvas. Keep text large and centred, since status updates are viewed briefly on phones, and export as PNG or JPG.

Are my photos uploaded when I make a grid or post?

No. Photos are opened and processed in your browser, and the tiles or designs are created on your device. Nothing is uploaded to a server, so you can work with client photos or unreleased product images privately.
