YouTube Tags Generator: Stay Within the 500-Character Limit

Generate YouTube tags from your own keywords, count them the way Studio does, and understand what tags actually do (and do not do) for your videos.

YouTube tags are descriptive keywords you can add to a video in YouTube Studio, with roughly 500 characters available in total. They are worth filling in, but they are not a secret ranking switch: YouTube itself says tags play a minimal role, mainly helping when people misspell your topic.

So the aim is a short, honest list that fits the limit, made quickly. The YouTube Tag Generator does exactly that from keywords you already know, without pretending to have search-volume data.

What tags do, and what they do not

Here is a fair picture of tags today:

  • They help with misspellings and alternate names. A video about "Rabindra Sangeet" could include "rabindrasangeet" and "tagore songs".
  • They clarify ambiguous topics. "Python" could be a snake or a programming language; a tag like "python programming" removes doubt.
  • They are mostly invisible to viewers. Tags do not appear on the watch page in the way hashtags do.
  • They do not replace a good title and description. YouTube relies far more on those, on the thumbnail, and on what viewers actually watch.

That is why the tool shows a clear note saying tags matter less than many creators think. Use them, but do not obsess over them.

Using the YouTube Tag Generator

  1. Open the YouTube Tag Generator.
  2. In Seed keywords, type your main phrases separated by commas or new lines, most important first. For example: excel pivot table, excel for beginners, data analysis.
  3. Optionally add a City / location (useful for local businesses, food or travel videos) and your Channel or brand name.
  4. Under Add variations, choose the modifiers you want. Singular / plural, "how to …", "… for beginners", "… tutorial" and "… tips" are on by default; you can also tick "… explained", "… review", "… in hindi", "… in bengali" and "… india".
  5. Press Generate tags.
  6. Review the list. Remove a tag with its × button, add your own in Add your own tag (press Enter or Add), and reorder by dragging the grip handle.
  7. Press Copy (comma-separated) and paste into the Tags field in YouTube Studio. TXT downloads the list, and Clear empties it.

On a keyboard you can focus any tag, move it with the arrow keys and remove it with Delete, which makes the tool usable without a mouse.

How the 500-character counter works

YouTube's limit is not just the sum of the letters in your tags. The generator counts:

  • every character in each tag,
  • 2 extra characters for any tag containing a space, because Studio wraps such tags in quotes,
  • 1 character for each comma between tags.

So excel tips costs 12, not 10. Each tag shows its own cost in small grey numbers, and the counter at the top reads something like "312 / 500 characters · 18 tags". Once the list goes past 500, the extra tags appear crossed out with a dashed border. Copy leaves those out automatically, and Trim to 500 deletes them from the list.

Because order decides what survives a trim, keep your most important tags at the top.

A sensible tag list in practice

For a Bengali cooking channel's video on "fish curry", a practical set might be:

  • fish curry, fish curries (singular and plural)
  • bengali fish curry, macher jhol (the dish's own name)
  • fish curry recipe, how to make fish curry
  • fish curry in bengali, fish curry kolkata
  • the channel name

That is around ten tags and well under the limit. Every one describes the video. You can see why the "in hindi", "in bengali" and city options exist: Indian viewers often add the language or place to their search.

Where to paste tags in YouTube Studio

Tags are easy to miss because YouTube keeps them below the fold. On a computer, open YouTube Studio, go to Content, select the video and open its Details page. Scroll down and click Show more; the Tags box sits in that expanded section. Paste the comma-separated list there and YouTube splits it into separate tags. Studio shows its own character counter under the box, which is worth a glance before saving.

During a fresh upload, the same box appears on the Details step, again under Show more. If you upload from a phone, tags may not be offered in the app, so adding them later from a computer is common.

One more practical point: when you copy, the generator sends only the tags that fit within 500 characters. If you need to keep a longer master list for future videos, use TXT to download every tag, including those over the limit, and trim per video.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Adding trending but unrelated terms. Tagging a phone review with a cricketer's name is misleading metadata and can break YouTube's spam policies.
  • Using a year in tags. The generator deliberately avoids years so tags do not go stale on evergreen videos.
  • Copying a competitor's entire tag list. Their video is not yours; only reuse terms that truly describe your content.
  • Putting hashtags in the tag field. Hashtags belong in the title or description; the tool strips leading # symbols from tags for this reason.
  • Spending more time on tags than on the thumbnail. The effort-to-impact ratio is poor.

Frequently asked questions

Do YouTube tags help my video rank?

Only a little. YouTube’s own help pages say tags play a minimal role in discovery and are most useful when your topic is commonly misspelled. Your title, thumbnail, description and how viewers respond to the video matter far more. Spend a minute on tags, not an hour.

Why does YouTube Studio show a different character count?

Studio counts the quotation marks it adds around tags that contain a space, plus the commas between tags. The BISCS counter follows the same approach, adding 2 for each multi-word tag and 1 for each comma, but it is still an approximation. If the two differ, trust Studio’s counter.

How many tags should I use on a YouTube video?

There is no ideal number. Five to fifteen focused tags covering the main topic, a few variations and any common misspellings are usually enough. Filling all 500 characters with loosely related phrases adds nothing and can look like misleading metadata.

Should I add my channel name as a tag?

It can help a little when people search for your channel by name, especially if the name is unusual or often misspelled. Enter it in the “Channel or brand name” field and the generator adds it at the end of the list.

What happens to tags past the 500-character limit?

The generator crosses them out and leaves them out when you press “Copy (comma-separated)”. You can drag the important ones higher, delete weak ones, or press “Trim to 500” to remove everything over the limit in one go.
