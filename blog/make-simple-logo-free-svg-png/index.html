A good small-business logo is simple: a clear name in a readable font, an optional symbol, and two or three colours used consistently. You do not need a complex illustration to look professional, especially when most people will see your logo as a tiny circle on WhatsApp or Instagram.

The Logo Maker from BISCS India helps you build exactly that kind of logo. Type your name, try a few ideas, adjust the icon, font, layout and colours, and download an SVG, transparent PNGs and a square app icon. It works in your browser and needs no account.

What makes a logo work?

Before opening any tool, it helps to know what you are aiming for:

  • Readable at small sizes. If the name is unreadable at 48 pixels wide, it will fail as a profile picture or favicon.
  • Simple shapes. One icon, not three. Thin, detailed drawings disappear when printed on a pen or a carry bag.
  • Limited colours. Two main colours are easier to reproduce on signboards, uniforms and printed bills.
  • Works in one colour. Your logo should still be recognisable in black on a photocopied invoice or a rubber stamp.
  • Fits your business. A playful rounded font suits a kids' activity centre; a classic serif suits a law chamber or jeweller.

How to make a logo step by step

  1. Open the Logo Maker and type your Brand name. Add a Tagline if you want one, such as "Chartered Accountants" or "Home Bakery, Pune".
  2. Look at the Logo ideas generated for your name. Press Generate new ideas for more, and click any idea to apply it as a starting point.
  3. Choose the Symbol: an Icon from the searchable library (type words like "leaf", "home" or "code"), your Initials, or None for a pure wordmark.
  4. Pick an Icon background: none, circle, rounded square or hexagon, and adjust the icon line weight.
  5. Choose a Layout: icon left, icon top, badge or wordmark.
  6. Under Typography, choose from ten font styles (from Inter and Georgia serif to Impact, Typewriter mono and Casual hand), set the weight, adjust letter spacing and optionally switch to UPPERCASE.
  7. Under Colours, try a ready palette (Indigo, Sunset, Ocean, Forest, Gold and more), or set your own brand colours, with an optional gradient fill.
  8. Check the previews: On light, On dark and on a sample business card.
  9. Download what you need: SVG (editable), PNG at 512, 1024 or 2048 px, a PNG for dark backgrounds, and a Favicon / app icon 512.

All rendering happens on your device, so a brand name you have not announced yet stays private.

The SVG keeps your text as editable text. That is handy for a designer, but if the viewing computer lacks the same font, the logo can look different. When in doubt, send the PNG. To create full favicon sets in several sizes from the 512 px icon, use the Favicon Generator.

Choosing colours and fonts with intent

Colours carry associations, though none are rules. Greens often suggest nature, health and agriculture; blues suggest trust and technology; warm oranges and reds feel energetic and food-friendly; gold and deep maroon feel premium and traditional, which is why they are popular with jewellers and wedding services.

For fonts, a clean sans serif like Inter reads well everywhere. Serif styles feel established and formal. Rounded fonts feel friendly. Use the bolder weights if your logo will appear small, since thin strokes fade on screens and in print.

Common logo mistakes

  • Copying a famous brand's look. A logo that resembles a well-known mark can lead to legal trouble and confuses customers. Make sure yours is clearly your own.
  • Using a long name in a tiny space. "Sri Lakshmi Venkateswara Textiles and Readymades" will not fit a profile circle. Use the icon or initials version for small placements.
  • Too many effects. Gradients, shadows and outlines together look dated quickly.
  • Low-resolution files. Never stretch a small PNG for a shop banner. Download the 2048 px version.
  • Ignoring dark backgrounds. A dark-blue logo vanishes on a black Instagram highlight cover. Use the dark-background PNG.
  • Changing it every few months. Recognition comes from consistency.

Practical tips for small businesses

Use your logo the same way everywhere: website header, Google Business Profile, WhatsApp Business photo, invoices and packaging. Keep the files in one shared folder so staff do not screenshot old versions.

If you plan to register a trademark, do a search first and consider professional advice, since a simple combination of a common icon and text may not be distinctive enough. Rules and fees change, so check the official IP India website for current requirements.

Once your logo is ready, drop it into a launch post with the Instagram Grid & Story Maker. Whenever you need a variation, such as a new tagline or a festive colour, reopen the Logo Maker and export a fresh set in minutes.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use a logo made with this tool for my business?

You can use the files you create, but you are responsible for making sure your logo does not copy or closely resemble another company’s mark. Icon-and-text logos built from common symbols may not be distinctive enough to register as a trademark, so consider a designer if registration matters to you.

Should I download SVG or PNG?

Use transparent PNG for websites, social media, documents and printing at normal sizes, because the fonts are baked in and it looks the same everywhere. Use SVG when a designer or printer needs an editable, scalable file, but note that its text uses fonts installed on each computer.

What size should a logo be?

Keep a high-resolution master. The 2048 px PNG works for print and large banners, 1024 px for websites and presentations, and 512 px for social profile pictures. The square 512 px app icon is designed for favicons, WhatsApp Business profile photos and app-style icons.

Can I register my logo as a trademark in India?

Trademarks in India are registered through the Controller General of Patents, Designs and Trade Marks. A trademark search is wise before you commit to a name or logo. Because rules and fees change, check the official IP India website or speak to a trademark professional.

Does the logo maker upload my brand name or designs?

No. The logo is drawn in your browser with SVG and canvas, and every download is created on your device. Your brand name, tagline and design choices are not sent to any server.
