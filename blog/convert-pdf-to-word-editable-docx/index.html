How to Convert PDF to Editable Word (DOCX) for Free

Turn a PDF into an editable Word file that keeps headings, lists and basic formatting, or an exact page-by-page copy. Scanned pages work too, via on-device OCR.

By BISCS India Editorial Team Updated 2 November 2026 5 min read
Quick answer

To convert a PDF to Word, open a PDF-to-Word converter, choose Editable text to get headings, paragraphs and lists you can edit, or Exact look to keep each page as an image, then download the DOCX. PDF to Word

Convert PDF text and basic layout into an editable Word (DOCX) file.

Open PDF to Word

To convert a PDF to Word, open PDF to Word, choose your PDF, pick Editable text (headings, paragraphs, lists, bold and italic you can edit) or Exact look (each page as a picture), and download the .docx file. Scanned pages can be read with OCR in English, Hindi or Bengali.

The conversion runs in your browser. Your PDF is never uploaded, which matters for contracts, salary letters and anything with personal details.

Two ways to convert, for two different needs

The most important choice is the mode. Picking the right one avoids disappointment.

Mode What you get Choose it when
Editable text Real Word text with headings, paragraphs, lists, bold and italic You need to change wording, reuse text or update a document
Exact look Each PDF page placed as an image in the Word file You need the document to look identical, for example to add notes around it

Editable mode deliberately simplifies complex layouts. Tables, multiple columns, text boxes and pictures are reduced to clean text, because recreating every design detail usually produces a Word file that is hard to edit. Exact look keeps appearance perfectly but the text cannot be edited.

How to convert PDF to Word step by step

  1. Open PDF to Word and choose your PDF, or drag it into the box.
  2. Pick Editable text or Exact look.
  3. In Editable mode, decide whether to Start each PDF page on a new page in Word.
  4. If the tool reports scanned pages, keep Recognise scanned pages (OCR) ticked and choose the OCR language: English, Hindi, Bengali, English + Hindi or English + Bengali.
  5. In Exact look, choose Image quality: Standard or High.
  6. Check the preview of the detected text, page by page.
  7. Convert and download your .docx file.

How it handles scanned PDFs

When a page has almost no selectable text, the tool marks it as scanned and tells you which pages are affected. With OCR switched on, it renders those pages and recognises the text on your device.

A few honest points about OCR:

  • The first run downloads the engine and language data to your browser once; later runs reuse it.
  • Accuracy depends on the scan. A clean, straight, well-lit page gives good results; a tilted phone photo with shadows gives more errors.
  • Proofread numbers and names. OCR can confuse similar shapes, such as 0 and O, or 1 and l.
  • Choose the right language. Ticking "English + Hindi" for a mixed document works better than English alone, but using only the languages present is faster and more accurate.

If your scans are phone photos, capturing them with Scan to PDF first (which straightens and cleans pages) noticeably improves OCR.

Where PDF to Word saves real time

Updating an old document. The original Word file of a company policy is lost, but the PDF survives. Convert it, update the clauses, and export a new PDF.

Reusing a resume. You have only a PDF of your CV. Convert it, edit the latest job and skills, and save again.

Editing a rent agreement draft. A landlord sends a PDF draft. Convert it, make your proposed changes visible in Word, and send it back for discussion.

Hindi or Bengali office letters. A scanned circular in Hindi needs to be quoted in a reply. OCR with the Hindi option recovers the text so you do not have to retype it.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Expecting a pixel-perfect editable copy. No converter can perfectly turn PDF layouts into editable Word. Use Editable text for content and Exact look for appearance.
  • Converting when you only need the words. If you just want the text, PDF to Text is quicker.
  • Skipping the proofread after OCR. Always read through scanned-page text, particularly amounts, dates and ID numbers.
  • Trying a password-protected PDF. Unlock it first by saving an unprotected copy with the password.
  • Converting a PDF just to fill in a form. Fillable forms are easier with Fill PDF Form, and flat forms with Edit PDF.

Digital PDF or scanned PDF: what to expect

Source PDF Text accuracy Speed Notes
Exported from Word, Google Docs or a website Exact, taken from the file Seconds Best results; headings and lists detected from font sizes and layout
Bank or government e-statement Exact Seconds Tables become text; use Exact look if the layout matters
Clean office scan Good with OCR Slower, page by page Proofread figures
Tilted phone photo saved as PDF Variable Slower Rescan with a document scanner for better OCR
Mixed (some pages scanned) Exact for digital pages, OCR for the rest Depends on scanned page count The tool lists which pages are scanned

Headings are worked out from the relative size and weight of the text, so a well-structured digital PDF turns into a Word document with proper Heading styles. That makes it easy to generate a table of contents or reorganise sections in Word afterwards.

Checklist after converting

  • Headings came through as headings, so Word's navigation pane works.
  • Lists are still lists, not lines starting with stray symbols.
  • Scanned pages have been read and proofread.
  • Page breaks fall where you want them.
  • Any tables or images that were simplified have been fixed or recreated if needed.

Converting back to PDF

After editing, save from Word as PDF, or use Word to PDF. Then you can merge, sign or compress the new PDF as needed.

Privacy and performance

Everything happens on your device: the PDF is read in your browser, OCR runs locally, and the Word file is assembled without any server. Long scanned documents take a while, since each page is recognised on your own processor, and a laptop is noticeably faster than an older phone. Digital PDFs with selectable text convert in seconds.

Frequently asked questions

Will my PDF look exactly the same in Word?

In Editable text mode, the text, headings, lists, bold and italic come through, but tables, columns, text boxes and pictures are simplified, so the layout will differ. If the appearance matters more than editing, choose Exact look, which places each page as an image in the Word file.

Can I convert a scanned PDF to Word?

Yes. Pages without selectable text are detected as scanned, and with the OCR option on (it is on by default) their text is recognised in English, Hindi, Bengali or a mix. Accuracy depends on scan quality, so proofread the result, especially numbers and names.

Why is the first OCR conversion slower?

The first time you use OCR, the recognition engine and language data download once to your browser. After that they are reused, so later conversions start faster. Digital PDFs with selectable text do not need OCR at all and convert quickly.

Does it convert tables into Word tables?

Not reliably. Editable mode focuses on text flow: headings, paragraphs and lists. Table content comes through as text, but borders and cell structure are simplified. For documents that are mostly tables, consider Exact look or recreating the table in Word.

Is my PDF uploaded during conversion?

No. Reading the PDF, recognising scanned pages and building the Word file all happen inside your browser, using libraries served from this site. Your document never leaves your device, so it suits confidential files.
