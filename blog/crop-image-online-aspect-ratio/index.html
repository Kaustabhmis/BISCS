Cropping an image means cutting away the parts you do not want, so the picture focuses on what matters or fits a particular shape. A crop can turn a cluttered group photo into a clean portrait, make a landscape image fit a square Instagram post, or remove a notification bar from a screenshot.

Which aspect ratio should you crop to?

An aspect ratio is simply width compared to height. Picking the right one before you crop saves you from having the platform crop your picture for you, often in the wrong place.

Aspect ratio Shape Common uses
Free Any Removing clutter, trimming screenshots, documents
Original Same as your photo Tightening a shot without changing its shape
1:1 Square Instagram posts, WhatsApp and LinkedIn profile photos, product thumbnails
4:3 and 3:4 Classic photo Standard prints, presentations, most phone cameras
3:2 DSLR photo 6 × 4 inch prints
16:9 Widescreen YouTube thumbnails, slides, desktop wallpapers
9:16 Tall Stories, Reels, Shorts, phone wallpapers
Passport 35:45 Portrait Passport and visa photos, many application forms

If a website asks for a specific size such as 1280 × 720, that is 16:9. A request for 600 × 600 is 1:1. Matching the ratio first means the later resize will not stretch or squash anything.

How to crop a photo step by step

  1. Open Crop Image and choose your photo, drop it on the page or paste it with Ctrl + V.
  2. Tap an Aspect ratio chip, or leave it on Free to crop any shape.
  3. Drag inside the frame to move it, and drag a corner or edge handle to resize it. The grid lines help you line up horizons and eyes using the rule of thirds.
  4. For precision, type values into the X, Y, Width and Height fields. On a keyboard, the arrow keys nudge the frame and Shift + arrows resize it.
  5. Check the Result preview, which shows the final output size in pixels.
  6. Download the cropped image.

Tips for a better crop

Use the rule of thirds

The grid divides the frame into nine parts. Placing a person’s eyes on the upper line, or the horizon on the lower or upper line rather than the middle, tends to look more natural. For product photos and passport-style shots, centring is better.

Leave breathing room

Cropping right up to the edge of someone’s head or chin looks cramped. Leave a little space above the head and keep the chin clear. If you are cropping for an official photo, follow the specific head-size guidance of the authority concerned.

Watch the pixel count

Every crop throws pixels away. A tight crop from a 12-megapixel photo is still large, but a tight crop from a WhatsApp image might leave only a few hundred pixels, which will look soft when printed or enlarged. The result preview tells you the final size before you download.

Straighten first

If the horizon is tilted, crop alone will not fix it. Straighten the photo with the fine-angle control in Rotate & Flip Image, then crop away the angled edges.

Cropping screenshots and documents

Crop is not only for photos. Free mode is ideal for tidying screenshots before you share them in a WhatsApp group, a presentation or a bug report. Trim away the status bar, the keyboard and any unrelated chats so the reader sees only what matters. It is also a simple privacy step: cropping removes other people’s names, phone numbers or notifications that happen to be on screen.

For photographed documents, such as a bill, a rent agreement or a mark sheet shot on a table, crop tightly to the paper edges so the background disappears. If the page was photographed at an angle, cropping cannot correct the perspective; the Scan to PDF tool detects the corners and flattens the page for you. A clean crop also shrinks the file, because the pixels you remove no longer need to be stored.

Cropping photos for application forms and ID cards

Many Indian application portals, from university admissions to recruitment exams, ask for a recent passport-style photograph with the face clearly visible and a plain light background. Before resizing and compressing to meet the portal’s limits, crop properly:

  • Use Passport 35:45 if the form describes a passport-size photograph, or the ratio implied by the dimensions in the notice.
  • Keep the full face, both ears where possible, and the top of the shoulders in view.
  • Make sure there is no other person, hand or object in the frame.

Then resize with Resize Image and compress with Compress Image to meet the exact numbers in the official notice. If you want a printable sheet of passport photos instead, the Passport Photo Maker is purpose-built for that.

Common cropping mistakes

  • Cropping in the wrong ratio and then forcing a resize. The picture gets stretched. Choose the ratio first.
  • Cutting off part of the chin or hair. Official photos are often rejected for this. Leave a margin.
  • Cropping a screenshot but leaving the status bar. Battery and time icons look untidy in documents and presentations.
  • Over-cropping a low-resolution image. A tiny crop from a forwarded photo will not print well.
  • Forgetting that the platform will crop again. If you upload a 4:3 image where 1:1 is expected, the site decides what to cut. Do it yourself.

Is it safe to crop personal photos here?

The cropping happens on your own device using your browser’s canvas. Your photo is not uploaded or stored anywhere, which is reassuring when you are trimming ID cards, certificates or family pictures. By default the output keeps the original file format, so a JPG stays a JPG, but you can switch to JPG, PNG or WebP and set the quality before downloading.

Frequently asked questions

Does cropping reduce the quality of a photo?

Cropping itself does not blur anything; it simply removes pixels outside the frame. The remaining part has fewer pixels in total, though, so if you crop a tiny area and then enlarge it for printing, it can look soft. Keep crops generous when you plan to print.

How do I crop a photo to a perfect square?

Choose the 1:1 aspect ratio chip. The crop frame locks to a square, so however you drag the corners it stays square. Move it over your subject and download. This is ideal for Instagram posts, profile pictures and product thumbnails.

Can I type exact crop coordinates?

Yes. Below the aspect ratio chips are X, Y, Width and Height fields in pixels. Typing values there moves and resizes the crop frame precisely, which helps when you need the same crop on several screenshots or an exact pixel size.

What is the difference between cropping and resizing?

Cropping cuts away part of the picture and changes what is shown. Resizing keeps the whole picture but changes how many pixels it uses. For an exact size like 600 × 600, crop to the right shape first, then resize to the right dimensions.
