With the BISCS India tool, all of this happens inside your browser: the file is read on your device and the PDF is created by your browser's own "Save as PDF" feature, so nothing is uploaded.

PDF is the safest format for sharing a document that should look the same everywhere and not be edited casually, which is why job portals, government offices and clients so often ask for it.

Why convert Word documents to PDF?

  • It looks the same on every device. A Word file can shift when opened in a different version or a phone app. A PDF stays put.
  • Portals often accept only PDF. Many job applications, college admissions and government forms require PDF uploads.
  • It is harder to edit by accident. A quotation, offer letter or notice in PDF discourages casual changes.
  • It opens anywhere. Every phone and computer can read a PDF without Microsoft Office.

How to convert Word to PDF online

  1. Open Word to PDF.
  2. Choose your .docx file or drag it onto the page.
  3. Check the preview. The panel shows the word count, headings, tables and images found, plus any conversion notes.
  4. Set up the page: Paper (A4 or Letter), Margins (Normal 2.54 cm, Moderate 1.9 cm or Narrow 1.27 cm), Body font and Base font size (10 to 12 pt).
  5. Click Download PDF. In the print dialog, choose Save as PDF as the destination.
  6. Under more settings, turn off Headers and footers for a clean page, then save.

The tool also offers an HTML export if you want the document as a web page. Your page setup choices are remembered on your device for next time.

In practice, letters, resumes, reports, assignments, notices, policies and most business documents convert cleanly. Brochures, newsletters and forms with complex layouts are better exported from Word itself.

Choosing page size, margins and font

Paper size. Use A4 for anything submitted in India; it is the standard for offices, courts, universities and government. Choose Letter only for documents going to the US or Canada.

Margins. Normal (2.54 cm, about one inch) suits most documents. Moderate fits more on each page, and Narrow is useful for tables and reference sheets, but can look cramped for letters.

Font. The tool offers a Calibri-style sans-serif, a Cambria-style serif, Times New Roman and Arial. Formal letters and legal documents often use Times New Roman; modern reports read well in a sans-serif. The preview shows an estimated page count, so you can see the effect of each choice.

Common problems and fixes

  • "Old .doc files are not supported." Open the file in Word, LibreOffice or Google Docs and save it as .docx.
  • "Could not convert this file." Check that the file is a genuine .docx and not password protected.
  • Date, time and web address printed on the page. Turn off Headers and footers in the print dialog.
  • Page breaks in odd places. Exact Word page breaks are not preserved. Try a different margin or font size, and check the preview's page estimate.
  • Missing header or footer text. Headers and footers from Word are not carried over. If your letterhead lives in the header, add it to the body of the document before converting.
  • The PDF is too large to upload. Documents with big photos make large PDFs. Shrink the result with Compress PDF.

A practical example: an offer letter for a small business

A small firm in Kolkata writes offer letters in Word but its HR person only has a laptop without Office.

  1. Open the .docx offer letter in the converter.
  2. Set A4, Normal margins and Times New Roman at 12 pt.
  3. Check that the salary table and signature block look right in the preview.
  4. Save as PDF with headers and footers turned off.
  5. Add the director's signature with Sign PDF and email it to the candidate.

Converting Word to PDF on a phone

Plenty of people receive a Word file on WhatsApp or email and need a PDF quickly, with no laptop nearby. The steps are the same on a phone, with a couple of differences:

  1. Save the .docx attachment to your phone's files or downloads.
  2. Open the converter in Chrome, Safari or another browser and choose the file.
  3. Check the preview by scrolling; pinch to zoom if needed.
  4. Tap Download PDF. On Android, pick Save as PDF as the printer; on an iPhone, use the share option in the print screen to save the PDF to Files.

Large documents with many images take a little longer to prepare on older phones, so give them a moment.

Privacy: why converting in the browser matters

Many online converters upload your file to a server, convert it there and send it back. That is a concern for offer letters, legal notices, medical reports and anything with personal details.

Word to PDF avoids that. The document is read and laid out inside your browser, and the PDF is produced by your browser's print engine. The only download is the conversion library, fetched once the first time you use the tool. Your document itself never leaves your device.

Frequently asked questions

Does the converter keep my exact Word layout?

It keeps the content and the main structure: headings, paragraphs, bold and italic text, lists, tables, links and images. Complex layout features such as text boxes, multiple columns, headers and footers, footnote placement and exact page breaks are simplified, so always check the preview before saving the PDF.

Can I convert an old .doc file?

No. Only the modern .docx format is supported. Open the .doc file in Microsoft Word, LibreOffice or Google Docs, save or download it as .docx, and then convert it here. Password-protected documents also need the password removed first.

Why does the PDF have a date and web address at the top?

That is the browser’s own header and footer option in the print dialog. When you choose Save as PDF, look for a setting called Headers and footers under More settings and turn it off for a clean page. The tool reminds you of this next to the page setup options.

Is my document uploaded to a server?

No. The converter reads the .docx file inside your browser and the PDF is produced by your browser’s print function. The document is not sent to BISCS India. The conversion library is downloaded once when you first use the tool, which needs an internet connection.

What is the best way to get a PDF that matches Word exactly?

If you have Microsoft Word, use File, Save As and choose PDF, or Export, Create PDF. Word renders its own layout most faithfully. This tool is best when you do not have Word installed, are on a phone or shared computer, or do not want to upload a private document to a website.
