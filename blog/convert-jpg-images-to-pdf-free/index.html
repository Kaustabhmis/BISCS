To convert JPG or other photos to PDF, open Images to PDF, add your images, drag them into order, pick a page size (A4, Letter or Fit image), orientation and margin, and download. Each image becomes one page of a single PDF.

JPG and PNG images are placed into the PDF exactly as they are, without re-compression, so quality is preserved. And because conversion happens in your browser, your photos are never uploaded.

Why convert photos to PDF?

Most of us now "scan" with a phone camera. But offices, banks and portals usually ask for PDFs, not a pile of JPGs. A single PDF is:

  • Easier to submit: one upload instead of five, in a fixed order.
  • Easier to read: the reviewer scrolls through pages instead of opening images one by one.
  • Printable: A4 pages with margins print cleanly on any office printer.
  • Professional: a neat PDF looks better than WhatsApp-forwarded photos.

Typical uses include the front and back of an ID card, handwritten assignment pages, signed forms photographed at home, medical prescriptions for an insurance claim, and receipts for expense reimbursement.

How to convert images to PDF step by step

  1. Open Images to PDF and choose your images (JPG, PNG or WebP). Select several at once or drag them in.
  2. Drag the thumbnails into the order you want. Each image will be one page.
  3. Rotate any sideways image with its rotate buttons, or remove images you added by mistake.
  4. Choose a Page size: A4, Letter, or Fit image.
  5. With A4 or Letter, choose Orientation: Auto, Portrait or Landscape.
  6. Choose a Margin: None, Small or Large.
  7. Create the PDF and download it.

Choosing page size, orientation and margins

Setting Option When to use it
Page size A4 Anything for Indian offices, portals and printing
Letter Documents for the US and some international recipients
Fit image Screenshots, posters, artwork; page matches the photo exactly
Orientation Auto Each page follows its image (landscape photo, landscape page)
Portrait / Landscape When every page must be the same orientation
Margin None Edge-to-edge images, maximum size on the page
Small A neat default for documents
Large When pages will be bound, stapled or annotated

Auto orientation is the right choice most of the time, because a landscape photo of a wide receipt gets a landscape page instead of being shrunk onto a portrait one.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Photographing documents at an angle. Tilted, shadowy photos look unprofessional and can be rejected. For document photos, Scan to PDF detects the page edges, straightens the perspective and cleans up the background before making the PDF.
  • Leaving images in random order. Files from your gallery are not always sorted the way you expect. Check the order of thumbnails before converting.
  • Expecting a small file from big photos. Because images are not re-compressed, ten 4 MB phone photos make a PDF of roughly 40 MB. Use Compress PDF or resize the photos first with the Image Resizer.
  • Mixing very different image sizes with Fit image. You will get pages of different sizes. Choose A4 for a uniform document.

Upload portals and file size

Indian application portals usually state both the accepted format and a maximum size for each upload, and both vary from portal to portal. Some ask for a PDF for documents but a JPG for your photograph and signature. Read the instructions for the specific upload field.

If the PDF is too large, you have two routes. Either shrink the photos before converting, which gives you the most control, or convert first and then compress the PDF. For document photos, the Strong compression level is usually a good balance of size and readability.

Tips for clear document photos

  • Use daylight or a bright, even room light; avoid the shadow of your phone falling on the paper.
  • Place the document on a dark, plain surface so its edges stand out.
  • Hold the phone parallel to the page, not at an angle.
  • Fill most of the frame with the document, but keep all four corners visible.
  • Tap to focus on the text before taking the photo.

Frequently asked questions

Can I combine multiple photos into one PDF?

Yes. Add as many images as you need, drag them into order and each image becomes one page of a single PDF. It is the usual way to send several document photos, such as front and back of an ID card, as one file.

Will converting to PDF reduce image quality?

No. JPG and PNG images are embedded as they are, without re-compression, so the quality matches your original photos. The PDF will therefore be about as large as the images combined. Compress it afterwards if a portal needs a smaller file.

Which page size should I choose?

Pick A4 for anything that may be printed or submitted to an Indian office or portal, because A4 is the standard paper size here. Choose Fit image when you want each page to match the photo exactly, for example for screenshots or artwork.

Can I convert an iPhone HEIC photo?

The tool accepts JPG, PNG and WebP. Most iPhones can share photos as JPG automatically, or you can change the camera format in settings. Alternatively, convert HEIC to JPG first using an image converter, then add the JPG files here.

How do I fix a sideways photo before converting?

Each image in the list has rotate left and rotate right buttons. Turn any sideways photo until it is upright, and the PDF page will follow. With Auto orientation, the page shape also matches the rotated image.
