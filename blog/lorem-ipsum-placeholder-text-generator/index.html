Lorem ipsum is placeholder text: scrambled Latin-looking words that fill a layout before the real content exists. Designers, developers and print shops use it to see how a page looks with text in place, without readers getting distracted by what the words say.

A lorem ipsum generator saves you hunting for the passage online. Choose how much you need, click once, and paste it into your mock-up.

Where does lorem ipsum come from?

The text is derived from a passage in a Latin work on ethics by the Roman statesman Cicero. Over time, typesetters cut words and syllables from it until it became nonsense that still looks like a natural language, with a realistic mix of short and long words.

That realism is why it outlived alternatives like repeating "text text text" or "content goes here". Repeated words create obvious patterns on the page, and readable English pulls people into reading rather than judging spacing, fonts and hierarchy.

When should you use placeholder text?

Lorem ipsum is the right choice when the layout is what you are testing. Good examples:

  • Website and app mock-ups in Figma, Canva or plain HTML before the copy is written.
  • WordPress or Shopify theme testing, to see how blog cards, product descriptions and footers cope with short and long text.
  • Print layouts for brochures, menus, wedding cards and visiting cards, so the client can approve the design first.
  • Developer testing: checking that a long paragraph wraps properly, that text does not overflow a button, and that a page still works with 10,000 words.
  • Presentation templates, where every slide needs plausible body text.

When real content works better

Placeholder text has real drawbacks, and experienced designers often avoid it for anything beyond early wireframes.

Situation Better choice Why
Final client review Real or near-final copy Clients judge the content, not the boxes
Hindi, Bengali or other Indic layouts Sample text in that script Indic scripts need more line height and different fonts
Forms, buttons, error messages Actual labels Wording drives usability
Product pages Real product names and prices Lengths vary hugely, for example "Tea" vs a 70-character title
Pages going live Never placeholder Unfinished pages hurt trust and search visibility

A practical middle ground is to use lorem ipsum for long body text, but real headings, button labels and prices from day one.

Common mistakes with dummy text

  • Publishing it by accident. It happens more often than you would think, especially in image alt text, meta descriptions and footer columns. Search for "lorem" before every launch.
  • Testing only the ideal length. Real content is messy. Generate one very short and one very long version of each block to see how the design handles both.
  • Forgetting other languages. If your site will serve Hindi or Tamil readers, Latin filler hides problems with font rendering and line breaks.
  • Copying from random websites. Some "lorem ipsum" pages include hidden characters or odd formatting. A clean generator avoids that, and you can run any pasted text through the Whitespace Cleaner if needed.

Tips for designers and developers

Match realistic lengths. If product descriptions on your marketplace are usually 40 to 80 words, generate 60 words rather than three full paragraphs. Use the word count below the output to check.

Use the HTML option for templates. With Wrap in HTML p tags ticked, each paragraph comes inside its own HTML paragraph element, ready to paste into a code editor or the HTML view of a content management system. The download is then saved as an .html file.

Preview formatted text quickly. Writing documentation or a README? Paste generated paragraphs into the Markdown Preview alongside some headings and lists to see the full page structure.

Check the numbers. When a design brief says "around 300 words per section", paste your mix of real and placeholder text into the Word Counter to confirm.

Lorem ipsum in Word, Docs and design apps

Some programs have their own shortcuts. In Microsoft Word, typing =lorem() on a new line and pressing Enter inserts placeholder paragraphs, and =rand() inserts readable English sample text instead. Design tools such as Figma and Canva offer plugins or built-in fillers too.

Those shortcuts are handy, but they tie you to one program and give you little control over length. A browser generator works the same way everywhere: on your office PC, on a client's laptop or on your phone while you are reviewing a layout. You decide the exact number of words or sentences, then paste the result into any app, email or code editor.

It also helps when several people work on one project. If the developer, designer and content writer all use the same generator with the same settings, a "three-paragraph block" means the same thing to everyone, and the mock-ups line up.

A quick checklist before you hand over a design

  • Every block of placeholder text is clearly marked as temporary in the brief.
  • Headings, buttons, prices and form labels use real wording.
  • Long and short versions of key blocks have both been tested.
  • Any Indian-language sections have been checked with real text in that script.
  • A final search for "lorem" and "ipsum" returns nothing on the live site.

When you are ready to replace the filler, open the Lorem Ipsum Generator one last time to compare lengths, so the real copy fits the space you designed for.

Frequently asked questions

What does lorem ipsum actually mean?

It does not mean anything as a whole. The text is a scrambled and shortened version of a passage from a Latin work on ethics by the Roman writer Cicero, with words cut and altered so it reads as nonsense. That is the point: readers are not distracted by meaning while they judge the layout.

Is it fine to publish a page that still has lorem ipsum?

No. Live pages with placeholder text look unfinished, confuse visitors and give search engines nothing useful to index. Before launch, search your site or document for the words lorem and ipsum to catch any blocks you missed, including image alt text and meta descriptions.

Can I get placeholder text with HTML tags?

Yes. Tick Wrap in HTML p tags and each paragraph is placed inside its own paragraph element, ready to paste into an HTML template or a CMS code view. The download button then saves an .html file instead of a plain text file.

How much placeholder text can I generate at once?

The generator allows up to 100 paragraphs, 1,000 sentences or 10,000 words in a single batch. That is more than enough for long-page mock-ups, print layouts or testing how a template handles very long content.

Should I use lorem ipsum or Hindi placeholder text for Indian designs?

If the final content will be in Hindi or another Indian language, test with real sample text in that script, not Latin filler. Devanagari and other Indic scripts have different line heights, word lengths and font needs, and lorem ipsum will not reveal those problems.
