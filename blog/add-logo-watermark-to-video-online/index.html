Add a Logo or Channel Name Watermark to a Video

Place your logo or @handle on a video in any corner, set its size, margin and opacity, and choose whether it shows throughout or only for part of the clip.

By BISCS India Editorial Team Updated 2 November 2026 4 min read
Quick answer

To watermark a video, place a small logo or text such as your channel handle in a corner, at partial opacity, so it identifies you without hiding the content. A good watermark is small, consistent and slightly see-through. It tells anyone who sees a reposted clip where it came from, without getting in the way of the video itself. A corner logo or a short @handle at 60 to 80 percent opacity does that job well.

The Video Watermark tool adds a logo image, a line of text, or both, to any video on your own device. A live preview shows the result before you export.

Why creators and businesses watermark videos

  • Credit travels with the clip. Videos are forwarded on WhatsApp and reposted on other pages; your name goes along.
  • Brand recall. A consistent mark in the same corner across all your videos builds familiarity.
  • Client previews. A visible "Draft" or "Sample" label discourages use of unpaid work.
  • Local businesses. A shop name and phone number on a product video means anyone who sees it knows whom to contact.

A watermark is not a security lock, and no visible mark can stop a determined copier. It mainly discourages lazy reposting and makes the original source clear.

How to add a watermark

  1. Open the Video Watermark tool and choose a video (MP4, MOV, WebM or MKV), or drag it in.
  2. To use a logo, press Choose logo image. A transparent PNG gives the cleanest look. Remove logo takes it off again.
  3. To add text, type it in the Text box, for example @YourChannel or a website. Hindi, Bengali and emoji are drawn correctly by your browser.
  4. Pick a Text colour and a Font: Sans (clean), Impact (bold), Rounded, Serif or Monospace. Keep Soft shadow (readable on any background) ticked unless you have a reason not to.
  5. Choose a Position from the nine options: the four corners, the middle of each edge, or Centre.
  6. Adjust Size (width of video) (4% to 60%, starting at 18%), Margin (space from the edge) and Opacity (10% to 100%, starting at 80%).
  7. Leave Show for the whole video ticked, or untick it and enter Show from and Until times.
  8. Check the preview, then press Add watermark. Download the MP4 when it is ready, or use Watermark another.

On a first run the browser fetches the video engine (about 32 MB) from this site and caches it. Your video and logo are processed locally and not uploaded.

Choosing size, position and opacity

Size. For a corner logo, 10% to 18% of the video width is a common range. Larger than about 25% starts to feel like an advert rather than a signature. Text watermarks can be slightly wider because letters are thinner than a logo.

Position. Bottom right is the traditional choice, but think about where platforms put their own interface. On Reels and Shorts, the right edge holds like and comment buttons and the bottom holds the caption, so a Top left or Top right mark is often more visible. On YouTube's regular player, the bottom bar appears when viewers pause or hover.

Margin. A margin of 3% to 5% keeps the mark away from the very edge, where phones with rounded corners or notches can hide it.

Opacity. 60% to 80% is visible without being heavy. Drop it to around 40% for a centre watermark on client previews.

Timing the watermark

Some creators only show their mark for the first few seconds, or reveal a larger one at the end. Untick Show for the whole video, then type times like 0:00 and 0:05. The tool checks that the end time comes after the start time before it runs.

A permanent small mark is the better protection against reposts, since clips are often cut from the middle of a video.

Mistakes to avoid

  • A JPG logo with a white box around it. Export your logo as a transparent PNG first.
  • Watermarking someone else's content. Adding your name to a video you do not own does not make it yours, and can mislead viewers.
  • Covering subtitles or faces. Check the preview at the moments where on-screen text appears.
  • Thin, light text with no shadow. It disappears over bright skies and white walls.
  • Changing position every video. Consistency is what makes a watermark recognisable.

Watermarks for Indian small businesses

Shops, tutors and home bakers increasingly sell through WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook, where videos are forwarded far beyond the first person you sent them to. A watermark with your business name and a phone number or handle means a forwarded product video can still bring a customer back to you. Keep it short: a name and one contact detail are enough. Place it in a top corner so it is not covered by the caption bar on Reels, and use a light colour with the soft shadow for clips filmed in bright shop lighting.

Quality notes

The picture is re-encoded to H.264 to draw the watermark, while AAC or MP3 audio is copied without change. Long or 4K videos take a while in a browser, especially on phones; trim them first if you only need part of the video. For still images, the Image Watermark tool applies the same idea to photos.

Frequently asked questions

What logo file works best as a video watermark?

A PNG with a transparent background. It sits cleanly over the video without a white or black rectangle around it. A JPG logo always carries its background box. Use a reasonably large source image, say 500 pixels wide or more, so it stays crisp when scaled to the size you choose.

Can I add both a logo and text?

Yes. Choose a logo image and type text in the Text box, and the tool places them side by side as one watermark. The combined mark then follows the same position, size, margin and opacity settings, which keeps the branding tidy.

Does a watermark stop people from stealing my video?

It discourages casual reposting and makes the source obvious, but it does not prevent copying. A determined person can crop or blur a corner mark. A centre watermark is harder to remove but more distracting. For serious misuse, platforms have copyright reporting processes.

Can I show the watermark only at the start or end?

Yes. Untick Show for the whole video, then type the Show from and Until times, such as 0:00 and 0:05. The watermark appears only between those times, which suits a short opening credit or an end card.

Will the watermark be in the right place on a vertical video?

Yes. Position, size and margin are calculated from the actual video dimensions, so the same settings work on a 1080×1920 vertical clip and a 1920×1080 landscape one. The preview shows exactly where it will sit before you export.
