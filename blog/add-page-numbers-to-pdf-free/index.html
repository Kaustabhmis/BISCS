How to Add Page Numbers to a PDF (Position, Format, Start)

Open Add Page Numbers

To add page numbers to a PDF, open Add Page Numbers, pick a position (for example bottom centre), a format such as Page 1 of N, the pages to number and the starting number, then download. A live preview shows exactly where the numbers will appear before you save.

The numbers are added as real text on each page, the rest of the document is left untouched, and the PDF is processed in your browser rather than uploaded.

Why page numbers matter more than you think

Page numbers are easy to forget until someone says "see page 14" and nobody can find it. They are especially useful for:

  • Legal and property bundles, where lawyers and officers refer to specific pages.
  • Project reports and dissertations, which colleges often require to be numbered.
  • Tender and bid documents, where every page must be traceable.
  • Printed handouts, so loose pages can be put back in order after a meeting.
  • Merged application bundles, where the reviewer needs to navigate a long file.

How to add page numbers step by step

  1. Open Add Page Numbers and choose your PDF.
  2. Pick a Position from the six spots: top left, top centre, top right, bottom left, bottom centre or bottom right.
  3. Choose a Format: 1, 2, 3, Page 1, Page 1 of N or 1 / N.
  4. In Pages to number, leave it empty for all pages, or type a range such as 2-20 to skip a cover page.
  5. Set Start at if the first numbered page should show something other than 1.
  6. Adjust Font size, Margin (in mm) and Colour if needed.
  7. Check the live preview, then add the numbers and download.

Choosing a format

Format Looks like Best for
1, 2, 3 7 Books, reports, clean minimal documents
Page 1 Page 7 Letters and formal documents
Page 1 of N Page 7 of 32 Contracts, bundles, anything where missing pages matter
1 / N 7 / 32 Presentations and handouts

"Page 1 of N" is the most useful for legal and official papers, because the reader can immediately tell whether a page is missing.

Skipping the cover and contents pages

Many reports start numbering after the cover and the table of contents. Suppose your PDF has a cover, two contents pages, and 40 pages of content (43 pages in total). To number the content starting at 1:

  • Type 4-43 in Pages to number.
  • Set Start at to 1.

The fourth page of the file now shows "1", and the cover and contents stay clean. For the Page 1 of N format, note that N is the total number of pages in the PDF.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Numbers overlapping the existing footer. Many documents already have a footer line or address. Check the preview and move the number, or increase the margin.
  • Numbering before the final page order is settled. If you later add, remove or reorder pages, the numbers will be wrong. Finish arranging with Organize PDF or Merge PDF first, then number last.
  • Tiny numbers. A font size below about 8 points is hard to read when printed. The default is a safe starting point.
  • Bright colours on formal documents. Grey or black looks professional. Save colour for internal drafts.
  • Margins too small for printing. Many printers cannot print right at the edge of the paper. A margin of 10 mm or more is safer for printed documents.

Tips for professional-looking numbering

  • Bottom centre is the most common position and suits almost any document.
  • For documents that will be bound on the left, bottom right or top right keeps numbers visible when pages are flipped.
  • Use a slightly lighter grey than the body text so numbers do not distract.
  • If you also need "Confidential" or "Draft" on every page, add a watermark with Watermark PDF before or after numbering.

Example: a numbered tender bundle

An engineering firm in Pune is submitting a tender. The bid documents run to 120 pages, assembled from technical sheets, financial statements and certificates. The tender instructions require every page to be numbered and the bundle to be indexed.

The bid manager merges the files in the required order, then numbers pages 2 to 120 in the Page 1 of N format at bottom right, keeping the cover page clean. The index on page 2 can now refer to exact page numbers, and the evaluator can check that nothing is missing at a glance. Where a tender requires digital signing of the final file, that step comes last, after numbering, as numbering changes the file.

Checklist before you download

  • The preview shows numbers in clear space on the first page, a middle page and the last page.
  • The first numbered page shows the number you expect.
  • Pages that should not be numbered (cover, blank separators) are excluded in the Pages box.
  • Font size is readable when printed, and the colour is suitably understated.
  • The page order is final, and you will not merge or reorder after numbering.

Numbering a merged bundle for a submission

Application bundles for scholarships, visas or court filings often combine documents that already carry their own page numbers, such as a bank statement that says "Page 2 of 6". Adding a second, document-wide number in a different corner, for example top right in the Page 7 of 32 format, gives the reviewer a single reference system for the whole bundle. Keep it small and grey so it does not compete with the original numbers.

Limits worth knowing

Numbers are drawn using the standard Helvetica PDF font, so the tool supports Western digits and the word "Page". It does not add Roman numerals or Indian-language numerals. It also numbers based on the page's position in the file, so it cannot read and continue an existing printed numbering scheme.

Private and quick

Adding numbers is a light task, so even long documents finish in seconds. The PDF is opened, numbered and saved inside your browser, and nothing is uploaded to a server. Close the tab and nothing remains anywhere except the file you downloaded.

Frequently asked questions

How do I skip the cover page when numbering?

Type the pages to number in the Pages box, for example 2 to the last page, and set Start at to 1 if you want the first numbered page to show 1. Leave the box empty to number every page from the beginning.

Can I use the Page 1 of N format?

Yes. The format options are 1, 2, 3; Page 1; Page 1 of N; and 1 / N. The total N is the number of pages in the document, which is what readers usually expect when a file is printed or shared.

Will page numbers cover existing text in the footer?

They can if the document already has text where the number goes. Use the live preview to check, then change the position (six positions are available), increase the margin, or reduce the font size until the number sits in clear space.

Can I add numbers in Hindi or other scripts?

The numbers are drawn with a standard PDF font, so they appear as Western digits and English words such as Page. Indian-language numerals and words are not supported in this tool at the moment.

Does adding page numbers change the rest of the PDF?

No. The numbers are added as a small text layer on top of each page. Existing text stays selectable, images keep their quality and the file size grows only slightly.
