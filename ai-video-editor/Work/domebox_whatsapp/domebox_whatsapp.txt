Running a growing business on WhatsApp means lost instructions and performance reviews that
turn into arguments. Domebox fixes this chaos by replacing chat groups with four strict
rules for team accountability. First, tasks are never truly done until the person who
assigns them verifies the work. This stops half-finished jobs from slipping through because
the worker can't just tick it off themselves. Second, it kills biased annual appraisals using
a system-calculated delegation scope. Every month, it objectively weighs how much work
an employee delivered on time against their rework rate. Third, managers are held accountable too.
If an approval sits on a manager's desk past the deadline, the manager loses points,
ensuring the worker is never punished for a slow checkup. Finally, your data is never held hostage.
If a company stops paying for their subscription, the workspace simply turns read-only, keeping
years of crucial audit logs safe and accessible. By tying every single job to a verified timestamp,
Domebox shifts your entire culture from constant chasing to objective delivery.
