# Editing Style Guide

> Template. Fill it in yourself, or put 3–5 reference videos in `References/` and use the
> "Style analysis" prompt in `PROMPTS.md` to have Claude draft it. Mark anything uncertain with **(?)**.

## Format
- Platform: Instagram Reels (also YouTube Shorts)
- Canvas: 1080×1920, vertical
- Target length: 30–60 s

## Pacing
- Cut style: _(e.g. jump cuts on every pause longer than 0.4 s)_
- Keep natural pauses that carry meaning: yes
- Average shot length: _(e.g. 2–4 s)_

## Hook (first 3 seconds)
- On-screen headline: _(e.g. one line, max 6 words, top third)_
- Opening move: _(e.g. start mid-sentence, no intro)_

## Captions
- Font: _(e.g. Montserrat ExtraBold)_
- Size: _(e.g. 70–90 px at 1080 wide)_
- Colours: text _#FFFFFF_, highlight word _#FF7A2F_, outline/shadow _#000000_
- Words per line: _(e.g. 2–4)_
- Position: _(e.g. lower-middle, clear of the face and the bottom 400 px)_
- Case: _(e.g. UPPERCASE / Sentence case)_
- Highlight style: _(e.g. current word in accent colour)_

## Motion
- Zooms: _(e.g. 1.0 → 1.15 punch-in on key points, max one every 5 s)_
- Emphasis words: _(e.g. pop-in keyword cards for numbers and tool names)_
- Transitions: _(e.g. hard cuts only)_
- Graphics: _(e.g. screen recordings / tool screenshots as proof)_

## Audio
- Voice: _(e.g. normalised to -14 LUFS, light noise reduction)_
- Music: _(licensed only; e.g. low under voice, -24 dB)_
- Sound effects: _(e.g. none / subtle whoosh on transitions)_

## Colour
- Grade: _(e.g. natural, slight warmth, no heavy LUTs)_

## Don'ts
- Constant movement or shaking text
- Captions covering the face or app buttons
- Effects that don't support the message
