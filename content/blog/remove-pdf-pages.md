To delete pages from a PDF, open [Remove PDF Pages](/tools/remove-pdf-pages/), click the pages you want to get rid of (or type them, like `2, 5-7`), and click **Remove pages**. You get a new PDF without those pages, and your original file stays as it was.

No software, no account and no upload. The work happens in your browser, which keeps personal documents on your own device.

## When removing pages is the right fix

Most PDFs we receive carry pages we do not need. Removing them makes documents shorter, smaller and easier for the next person to read. Common cases:

- **Blank pages from double-sided scanning.** Scanners and photocopiers often add an empty back page after every sheet.
- **Terms and conditions pages.** Bank and insurance PDFs often end with several pages of fine print that a reviewer does not need.
- **Duplicate pages.** It happens when you scan the same sheet twice or merge overlapping files.
- **Sensitive pages.** Sharing a property document with a contractor? Drop the pages with family details or financial information.
- **Cover pages and adverts.** Some e-statements include promotional pages that only bulk up the file.

## How to remove pages step by step

1. Open [Remove PDF Pages](/tools/remove-pdf-pages/) and choose your PDF.
2. Wait for the page thumbnails to load.
3. Click each page you want to remove. Selected pages are marked clearly, and a counter shows how many will go.
4. Alternatively, type the pages in the **Pages to remove** box, such as `1, 3, 10-12`. The thumbnails stay in sync with what you type.
5. Read the status line: it tells you how many pages the new PDF will have.
6. Click **Remove pages** and download the new file.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- **Confusing printed page numbers with PDF positions.** If page 1 of the PDF is a cover, the page printed as "3" might be the fourth page in the file. Go by the thumbnails, not the numbers in the footer.
- **Deleting a page with a signature or stamp.** Some agreements are only valid with every signed page attached. Check before removing anything from a legal document.
- **Not checking the result.** Open the downloaded file and scroll through it quickly. It takes ten seconds and avoids sending an incomplete document.
- **Overwriting the only copy.** The tool never changes your original, but if you then delete the original from your phone yourself, the removed pages are gone for good.

## Removing pages before a portal upload

When an application portal limits file size, removing unnecessary pages is often the easiest way to get under the limit without lowering quality. A scanned page can easily be several hundred KB, so dropping three blank pages may be all you need.

Limits differ from portal to portal (and sometimes between documents on the same portal), so read the specific instructions. If the file is still too big after removing pages, use [Compress PDF](/tools/compress-pdf/) on the result.

## A note on sensitive information

Removing a page really removes it: the new PDF is built only from the pages you keep, so the deleted pages are not hidden inside. That makes page removal a safer way to withhold information than covering it with a white box.

However, information on the pages you keep stays as it is. If a page you need contains one sensitive line, such as a full account number, removing the page is not an option. In that case, recreate the document from its source, or use proper redaction software that permanently removes the text.

## Example: cleaning up a scanned property file

Meera in Hyderabad scanned her flat's purchase documents on the office copier to send to a bank for a top-up loan. The copier was set to double-sided, so the 18-page bundle came out as 36 pages, with a blank or near-blank back after most sheets. It also picked up the copier's own cover sheet as page 1.

She opens the PDF, clicks page 1 and each blank back page while scanning the thumbnails, and the counter shows 19 pages selected. The status line says the new PDF will have 17 pages. That does not match the 18 sheets she scanned, so she looks again and spots that she clicked one page with a faint stamp on it. She deselects it, the count becomes 18, and she downloads a file that is half the original size.

That small check, comparing the final page count with what you expect, is the best habit you can build with this tool.

## Tips for working faster

- Zoom out in your browser (Ctrl and minus) to see more thumbnails at once in long documents.
- Use ranges for patterns. To drop the last ten pages of a 60-page file, type `51-60`.
- If every other page is blank (a common double-sided scanning result), list them as ranges or click them one by one; then confirm the count before saving.
- On mobile, tapping thumbnails is usually quicker than typing.

## Private by design

The PDF is opened by a library running inside your browser tab. Selecting pages, building the new file and saving it all happen on your device, and nothing is uploaded or stored on a server. The first visit downloads the tool's code; after that, the processing needs no internet connection at all.
