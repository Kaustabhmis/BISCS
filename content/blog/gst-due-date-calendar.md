For a regular GST taxpayer who files monthly, the two key deadlines are **GSTR-1 by the 11th** and **GSTR-3B by the 20th** of the following month. Under the quarterly QRMP scheme, GSTR-1 is due by the **13th** of the month after the quarter, and GSTR-3B by the **22nd or 24th**, depending on your state. Composition taxpayers pay through **CMP-08 by the 18th** after each quarter and file **GSTR-4 by 30 April** after the year.

These are the standard dates. The government frequently extends them by notification, so always confirm on the GST portal. The free [GST Due Date Calendar](/tools/gst-due-date-calendar/) builds your full-year schedule from these rules and lets you download it as calendar reminders.

## GST due dates at a glance

| Return or payment | Who files | Standard due date |
|---|---|---|
| GSTR-1 (monthly) | Regular taxpayers filing monthly | 11th of the next month |
| GSTR-3B (monthly) | Regular taxpayers filing monthly | 20th of the next month |
| IFF (optional) | QRMP taxpayers, months 1 and 2 of a quarter | 13th of the next month |
| PMT-06 | QRMP taxpayers, months 1 and 2 of a quarter | 25th of the next month |
| GSTR-1 (quarterly) | QRMP taxpayers | 13th of the month after the quarter |
| GSTR-3B (quarterly) | QRMP taxpayers | 22nd (Category I) or 24th (Category II) |
| CMP-08 | Composition taxpayers | 18th of the month after the quarter |
| GSTR-4 | Composition taxpayers | 30 April after the financial year |
| GSTR-9 and GSTR-9C | Regular taxpayers, subject to turnover thresholds | 31 December after the financial year |
| GSTR-7 / GSTR-8 | TDS deductors / e-commerce operators collecting TCS | 10th of the next month |
| ITC-04 | Principals sending goods for job work | 25 April (annual), or 25 October and 25 April (half-yearly, turnover above ₹5 crore) |

## Which filing type applies to you?

- **Monthly:** the default for regular taxpayers, and compulsory if your aggregate turnover is above ₹5 crore.
- **QRMP:** an option for taxpayers with aggregate turnover up to ₹5 crore. Returns are quarterly, but tax is still paid monthly through PMT-06.
- **Composition:** a simpler scheme for eligible small businesses that pay tax at a fixed rate on turnover and cannot claim input tax credit.

If you are unsure which applies, your registration details on the GST portal show your status.

## Tips to never miss a GST deadline

1. **Import the .ics file at the start of each financial year**, and again if you change filing type.
2. **Keep the two-day reminder** so you have time to reconcile purchases and collect invoices.
3. **File GSTR-1 on time even if GSTR-3B is later.** Your B2B customers rely on it for their input tax credit.
4. **Validate customer GSTINs** in your sales data before filing with the [GSTIN Validator](/tools/gstin-validator/).
5. **Use consistent HSN or SAC codes** on invoices; the [HSN & SAC Code Finder](/tools/hsn-code-finder/) helps.
6. **Check for extensions** on the GST portal's news and updates section a few days before each date.

## Common mistakes

- Treating QRMP as "pay quarterly". Tax for the first two months is still due by the 25th through PMT-06.
- Using the 22nd when your state is in Category II, or vice versa.
- Assuming a date that falls on a Sunday moves automatically. It does not unless notified.
- Forgetting GSTR-7 or GSTR-8 obligations if you deduct TDS or run an e-commerce platform.
- Leaving the annual return to the last week of December, when the portal is busiest.
