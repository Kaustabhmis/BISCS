It is the quickest fix for the photos most of us take every day, such as dim indoor shots, yellowish pictures under tube lights, and faded prints scanned from a family album.

## What does the enhancer actually fix?

| Problem | Control that fixes it | How it works |
|---|---|---|
| Flat, greyish photo | Auto levels, Contrast | Stretches the darkest and brightest tones to use the full range |
| Too dark or too bright | Brightness | Lifts or lowers mid-tones |
| Yellow, green or blue tint | Auto white balance, Warmth | Neutralises the colour cast from lighting |
| Dull colours | Vibrance, Saturation | Vibrance boosts muted colours more than already-strong ones |
| Grain from low light | Noise reduction | Smooths noise while keeping edges |
| Dust and specks on scans | Median 3×3 | Removes tiny isolated spots |
| Soft detail | Sharpen | Increases edge contrast |
| Too small | Upscale 2× with AI | Neural super-resolution, optional |

This is careful, conventional image processing, not generative AI. It does not repaint faces or invent detail, which means your photos still look like your photos.

## Restoring old family photographs

Many Indian homes have albums of prints from the 1970s to the 1990s that have faded, turned yellow or pink, and collected dust. A careful digitising workflow gets the best out of them:

1. **Scan or photograph the print flat.** Use a scanner if you have one. Otherwise, photograph the print in daylight, holding the phone directly above it to avoid reflections. The [Scan to PDF](/tools/scan-to-pdf/) tool can detect the edges and flatten the perspective.
2. **Straighten and crop** away the album page and borders.
3. **Apply the Old photo preset.** It removes dust and noise, fixes fading and corrects colour casts.
4. **Adjust warmth.** Some old prints have a pleasant warm tone you may want to keep; others have a strong magenta cast you will want to remove.
5. **Remove scratches or spots** with the [Smart Object Eraser](/tools/object-eraser/).
6. **Upscale small prints** with the built-in 2× option or the [AI Image Upscaler](/tools/ai-image-upscaler/).

Keep the original scan as well. Your taste in editing may change, and an untouched master lets you start again.

## Fixing everyday lighting problems

Some lighting situations come up again and again in Indian homes and events, and each has a typical fix:

- **Tube lights and CFLs** often give a slightly green or blue tint. Raise **Auto white balance** and nudge **Warmth** up a little.
- **Yellow bulbs at weddings and pujas** turn everything orange. Lower **Warmth** and let auto white balance neutralise the cast, but stop before the scene loses its festive glow.
- **Diya-lit or candle-lit Diwali photos** are dark and grainy. Increase **Brightness** gently and use **Light** or **Strong** noise reduction. Do not expect daylight clarity from a photo lit by a few flames.
- **Backlit photos against a window** leave faces dark. Brightness and auto levels help, though a very bright window may stay washed out.
- **Hazy outdoor shots** in winter look flat. Auto levels and a touch of contrast restore depth.

## Tips for natural-looking results

- **Faces first.** Judge the result by skin tones. If faces look orange or grey, adjust warmth and white balance before anything else.
- **Lower sharpening for portraits.** Strong sharpening emphasises pores and wrinkles. Landscapes and buildings can take more.
- **Use Strong noise reduction only for very grainy photos.** It smooths fine texture such as fabric patterns and hair.
- **Vibrance before saturation.** Vibrance protects skin tones while lifting dull backgrounds; saturation boosts everything equally.
- **Check on another screen.** A photo that looks perfect on a bright phone may look too dark on a laptop.

## Common mistakes

- **Pushing every slider to the right.** Enhancement should be invisible; if people notice the editing, it is too much.
- **Enhancing a heavily compressed forward.** Blocky compression artefacts get sharpened too. Find the original where possible.
- **Expecting blur from camera shake to disappear.** Sharpening improves softness, but cannot undo motion blur.
- **Saving over the original file.** Always keep the untouched version.
