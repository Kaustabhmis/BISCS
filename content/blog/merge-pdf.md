To merge PDF files into one, open [Merge PDF](/tools/merge-pdf/), add two or more PDFs, drag them into the order you want and click merge. The combined file downloads straight away, and every page keeps its original size and quality.

The whole job happens in your browser. Your files are read and joined on your own device and are never uploaded, which is exactly what you want when the PDFs are bank statements, Form 16s or identity documents.

## Why people merge PDFs

Almost every application in India now ends with an upload step, and many portals allow only one file per section. Merging solves that. A few typical cases:

- **Loan or credit card applications**: six months of bank statements, three salary slips and an address proof, sent as a single PDF.
- **Visa and passport paperwork**: covering letter, itinerary, hotel bookings and ITR acknowledgements in one file.
- **College admissions**: mark sheets for Class 10, Class 12 and each semester combined into one "educational documents" upload.
- **Office reporting**: weekly reports from different branches joined into one pack for the regional manager.
- **Tax filing**: Form 16, Form 26AS and investment proofs bundled for your CA.

## How to merge PDFs step by step

1. Open [Merge PDF](/tools/merge-pdf/) and choose your files. You can select several at once or drag them into the box.
2. Add more files any time using the **Add more PDFs** area.
3. Arrange the order. Drag a file by its handle, or use the arrow buttons. Files are merged from top to bottom.
4. Remove any file you added by mistake.
5. Click the merge button. A progress bar shows each file being added.
6. Download the merged PDF. The result screen shows the total pages and how many files were joined.

## A checklist before you merge

Getting the order and contents right first saves you from redoing the job.

- Rename files with numbers (01-cover, 02-statement, 03-payslip) so they are easy to sort.
- Rotate any sideways scans first, because pages are merged exactly as they are and a sideways page stays sideways.
- Delete blank or irrelevant pages before merging, or afterwards with [Remove PDF Pages](/tools/remove-pdf-pages/).
- Fill in any interactive forms before merging, since form fields are not carried over.
- Check whether the portal has a file size limit, and keep an eye on the total size of what you are adding.

## Government portal uploads: size and format

Indian government and institutional portals often set a maximum file size per upload, and the limit varies a great deal between portals and even between sections of the same form. Some accept only a few hundred KB, others a few MB. There is no single national rule, so always read the instructions on the specific portal you are using.

Merging never makes files smaller; the merged PDF is roughly the sum of its parts. If you go over the limit, run the result through [Compress PDF](/tools/compress-pdf/). For scanned documents, the Strong level usually brings the size down a lot while keeping text readable.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- **Wrong order.** Reviewers rarely search through a file. Put the most important document first, or follow the order the portal lists.
- **Mixing portrait and landscape carelessly.** It is fine to combine them, but make sure each page is upright.
- **Merging locked PDFs.** Password-protected files are flagged with an "encrypted" label in the file list. Save an unlocked copy first.
- **Merging photos instead of PDFs.** If some documents are JPG photos from your phone, convert them with [Images to PDF](/tools/jpg-to-pdf/) first, then merge.
- **Expecting bookmarks to survive.** Outline bookmarks and form fields are dropped. The pages themselves are untouched.

## Merge vs organise: which tool do you need?

| You want to… | Use |
|---|---|
| Join whole files end to end | Merge PDF |
| Join files and then move individual pages around, rotate or delete some | Organize PDF (it has an "Add PDF" button) |
| Take a few pages from one file into a new file | Extract PDF Pages |

Merge is the quickest when each file already contains the right pages in the right order. If you need page-by-page control, start with [Organize PDF](/tools/organize-pdf/) instead.

## Example: building a home loan document pack

Suppose Priya in Bengaluru is applying for a home loan. The bank's checklist asks for KYC, income proof and bank statements, and the online form has a single "Supporting documents" upload. Her files are scattered: a PAN card PDF from DigiLocker, an Aadhaar PDF, three monthly payslips from the HR portal, and a six-month statement from net banking.

She opens the merger, adds all six files, and orders them the way the checklist does: PAN, Aadhaar, payslips from oldest to newest, then the statement. One click later she has a single 22-page PDF. Because the statement was a long digital file, the result is still small enough for the portal. Had it been a stack of scans, she would have compressed it before uploading.

The point is that the order mirrors what the reviewer expects to see. A loan officer reading a well-ordered file can verify it quickly, which tends to mean fewer follow-up calls asking for "the missing document" that was actually on page 17.

## What happens to file names and page sizes?

Each page keeps its own size, so an A4 payslip and a Letter-size US document can sit side by side in the same PDF without being stretched. Portrait and landscape pages also keep their orientation.

The output takes its name from the first file in the list, with "-merged" on the end. If you put the cover letter first, the result is named after the cover letter, which is often a sensible default.

## Tips for large batches

On a phone, merging ten small PDFs takes a few seconds. Very large scanned files, such as a 100-page property document, need more memory. If your phone struggles, try a laptop, close other heavy tabs, or merge in two stages: combine half the files, then merge that result with the rest.

The merged file is named after the first PDF with "-merged" added. Rename it to something meaningful, like `Rahul-Sharma-KYC-documents.pdf`, before uploading, because reviewers see the filename.

## Your files stay private

Many free PDF websites upload your documents, process them on their servers and then delete them, or say they do. With browser-based processing there is nothing to delete, because nothing was sent. The page loads the PDF library once, and from then on the merging happens on your device, even if your connection drops midway.
