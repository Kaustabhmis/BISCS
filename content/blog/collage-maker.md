A photo collage puts several pictures into one image, so you can share a whole event, a product range or a before-and-after in a single post. Making one should take a minute, not an evening with desktop software.

The [Collage Maker](/tools/collage-maker/) from BISCS India lets you pick 2 to 9 photos, choose from ready-made grid layouts or arrange them freely, and download a high-resolution JPG or PNG. It runs entirely in your browser, so your photos stay on your phone or computer.

## Grid or freestyle: which collage style should you choose?

The tool has two modes, and they suit different jobs.

| | Grid mode | Freestyle mode |
|---|---|---|
| Look | Clean, aligned cells | Scattered photos like prints on a table |
| Best for | Product catalogues, before/after, event recaps, real estate | Birthdays, travel memories, scrapbook-style posts |
| Control | Choose a layout, then zoom and position each photo within its cell | Move, resize and rotate each photo; send to back |
| Extras | Spacing, outer border, corner radius | Photo frames such as Polaroid, plus "Scatter all" |

Both modes support a background (solid, gradient, photo or transparent) and text captions.

## How to make a collage step by step

1. Open the [Collage Maker](/tools/collage-maker/) and choose 2 to 9 photos, or drag them onto the page.
2. Pick **Grid** or **Freestyle** mode.
3. In grid mode, choose a layout. There are over 25, from "Side by side" and "Stacked" to "1 big + 3 small", "Brick mosaic", "Hero + 6" and "3 × 3 grid".
4. Choose a **Canvas size**: Square 1:1, Portrait 4:5, Story 9:16, Landscape 16:9, A4 portrait, A4 landscape or a custom ratio.
5. Tap a cell to select it, then use **Zoom** and drag to frame the photo. Drag one photo onto another to swap them, or use **Replace** to change it.
6. Adjust **Spacing**, **Outer border** and **Corner radius**, and choose a background colour or gradient.
7. Use **Add text** for a caption. Pick a font, colour and size, make it bold, or add a background box so it stays readable on busy photos.
8. Under **Export**, pick a size (1080 px up to 4320 px on the long side) and JPG or PNG, then press **Download collage**.

Your photos are processed with the browser's canvas and never leave your device. Very large photos are scaled for editing to keep things smooth on phones, and the final export is rendered at the size you choose.

## Picking the right layout for your photos

The layout should match the story you are telling:

- **Before and after** (a renovated kitchen, a haircut, a cleaned sofa): use "Side by side" for landscape photos or "Stacked" for portrait ones.
- **One hero and details** (a saree with close-ups of the border and pallu, a cake with slices): "1 big + 2 small" or "1 big + 3 small" gives the main photo room while the details support it.
- **Event recap** (a wedding, school annual day, office Diwali party): "3 × 3 grid" or "Mosaic 9" fits many moments without feeling cramped.
- **Product range**: "2 × 2 grid" or "3 × 2 grid" keeps every item the same size, which feels fair and organised.
- **Property listing**: "Hero + 6" puts the exterior or living room up top and rooms below.

If a face or product is cut off, select that cell and drag the photo or reduce the zoom.

## Sizes for Instagram, WhatsApp and print

- **Instagram feed:** Portrait 4:5 shows larger in the feed than a square. Square 1:1 suits a uniform grid.
- **Instagram and WhatsApp status:** Story 9:16. Keep text away from the very top and bottom, where app buttons appear.
- **YouTube or presentations:** Landscape 16:9.
- **Printing:** A4 portrait or landscape, exported at 3508 px on the long side (A4 at 300 dpi). Many neighbourhood print shops accept a PNG or high-quality JPG on a pen drive or WhatsApp.

If you want to split one wide photo into a seamless Instagram grid instead, use the [Instagram Grid & Story Maker](/tools/social-post-maker/).

## Common collage mistakes

- **Too many photos.** Nine small pictures on a phone screen can look like clutter. Five or six well-chosen ones often work better.
- **Mixed lighting.** A collage of warm indoor photos and cold daylight ones looks uneven. Apply the same look to all of them first with [Image Filters](/tools/image-filters/).
- **Heads cut off at cell edges.** Always check each cell after choosing a layout.
- **Text over faces.** Put captions in a corner or on a background box.
- **Exporting too small.** 1080 px is fine for WhatsApp, but use 2160 px or more for Instagram and anything that might be printed.
- **Heavy files for sharing.** A 4320 px PNG can be several megabytes. If you need a lighter file, run it through the [Image Compressor](/tools/image-compressor/).

## Tips for collages that look professional

- Use consistent spacing and a plain background colour that appears in the photos, such as white for a clean catalogue or a soft cream for wedding pictures.
- Rounded corners and a little spacing suit lifestyle and food photos; zero spacing with square corners suits architecture and products.
- In freestyle mode, the Polaroid frame with slight rotation gives a warm, nostalgic feel for birthdays and travel albums.
- Keep captions short: a name, a date or a single line such as "Annual Day 2026, Class 5B".
- Export once as PNG for archiving and once as JPG for sharing.
