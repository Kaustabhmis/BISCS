Yes, you can edit a PDF for free without installing software. Open the file in [Edit PDF](/tools/pdf-editor/), choose a tool such as Add text, Whiteout, Highlight or Tick, click where you want the change, and press **Apply & download**. The edited PDF is saved straight to your device.

Because the editor runs entirely in your browser, the PDF is never sent to a server. That matters when the file is a salary slip, a bank statement or a form carrying your PAN or Aadhaar number.

## What can you actually change in a PDF?

PDF was designed as a "final" format, so editing works differently from Word. Instead of reflowing pages, a PDF editor adds new layers on top of what is already there. The BISCS editor gives you these tools:

- **Edit text**: click any existing line to replace it. Handy for a wrong date, a misspelt name or a changed amount.
- **Add text**: type anywhere on the page in Helvetica, Times or Courier, with size, bold, italic and colour options.
- **Sign**: draw, type or upload a signature and place it (the same feature powers the separate [Sign PDF](/tools/sign-pdf/) tool).
- **Image**: drop in a logo, a stamp or a photo.
- **Whiteout**: cover something with a white box.
- **Highlight, Rectangle, Ellipse and Line**: mark up a document for review.
- **Draw**: freehand pen for notes and circles.
- **Tick**: one-click check marks for forms that are not fillable.

Undo and redo are available (Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y), and you can zoom in for precise placement.

## How to edit a PDF step by step

1. Open [Edit PDF](/tools/pdf-editor/) and choose your file, or drag it into the box.
2. Wait a moment while the pages render. Thumbnails appear on the side so you can jump between pages.
3. Pick a tool from the toolbar. Each tool has a short hint at the top explaining what to do.
4. Click on the page to place text, a tick or a shape. For whiteout, highlight and rectangles, drag to draw the area.
5. Select any item to move it, resize it from the corners, duplicate it or delete it.
6. Use **Edit text** if you need to change a line that is already printed in the PDF.
7. Press **Apply & download**. Your edited copy downloads; the original file on your device is untouched.

## Everyday situations where a quick edit helps

**Filling a non-fillable form.** Many college admission forms, society NOC letters and older bank forms are scanned PDFs with no typing fields. Use Add text for your details and Tick for checkboxes, then print or email the result.

**Correcting an invoice before sending.** Spotted that the invoice says "Kolkata 700019" when it should be 700091? Edit text fixes the line without regenerating the whole document. For amounts, type "Rs." rather than the ₹ symbol, because the standard PDF fonts do not include it.

**Reviewing a draft.** A manager in Pune can highlight clauses, circle a figure and add a short comment, then send the file back on WhatsApp or email.

**Adding a company stamp or logo.** Upload a PNG of your stamp with the Image tool and place it next to the signature line.

## Edit text vs Add text vs Whiteout

| Tool | What it does | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Edit text | Covers an existing line and replaces it with your new text | Fixing typos, dates, names, numbers |
| Add text | Places new text anywhere | Filling blanks, adding notes |
| Whiteout | Draws a white box over content | Hiding stray marks or old details visually |

The key point: **Edit text and Whiteout hide content visually; they do not erase it from the file.** Someone with the right software may still be able to select or extract the covered text. For truly confidential redaction (for example, hiding an account number before sharing a statement publicly), recreate the document from its source or use dedicated redaction software.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- **Using Indian-language text or the ₹ sign.** The editor uses standard PDF fonts, which cover English and Western characters only. Unsupported characters are replaced with "?" when you save, and the tool warns you if this happens.
- **Treating whiteout as secure deletion.** As above, it is a visual cover only.
- **Editing a password-protected PDF.** The editor cannot open locked files. Open the PDF with its password in a reader, save an unprotected copy, and edit that.
- **Forgetting to check every page.** Scroll through the thumbnails before downloading so nothing lands on the wrong page.
- **Overwriting your only copy.** Keep the original until you have checked the edited version.

## Practical tips for neat results

- Zoom in to 150% or more when placing text on narrow form lines. It is much easier to line things up.
- Match the existing font roughly: Times for formal letters and government forms, Helvetica for modern documents, Courier for typewriter-style forms.
- Use the colour picker sparingly. Black or dark blue looks most natural on official documents.
- Duplicate an item (Ctrl+D) when you need the same text or tick in several places.
- If the PDF is a phone photo of a paper form, consider making a clean copy first with [Scan to PDF](/tools/scan-to-pdf/), which straightens and sharpens the page.

## When should you use a different tool?

If the PDF has real form fields (you can click into boxes in a PDF reader), [Fill PDF Form](/tools/fill-pdf-form/) is faster and fills the actual fields. If you want to rewrite whole paragraphs, convert the file with [PDF to Word](/tools/pdf-to-word/), edit it there, and export a fresh PDF. And if you only need to delete or reorder pages rather than change their content, a page tool is simpler.

## Privacy: your PDF stays on your device

Most free online editors ask you to upload the document to their servers. This one does not. The page loads the editing code into your browser, and from then on all rendering and saving happens on your own phone or computer. Nothing is stored after you close the tab, which is the sensible way to handle ID proofs, payslips and contracts.

The trade-off is that your device does the work. A 200-page scanned report will open more slowly on a budget phone than on a laptop, but your data never leaves your hands.
