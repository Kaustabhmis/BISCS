To rotate PDF pages permanently, open [Rotate PDF](/tools/rotate-pdf/), select the pages that need turning (or leave none selected to rotate all of them), press **Left**, **Right** or **180°**, and download the file. The new orientation is saved into the PDF, so it opens the right way up for everyone.

That last part matters. Rotating a page in a PDF viewer usually changes only your view, not the file, which is why the document is sideways again when someone else opens it.

## Why PDFs end up sideways

Sideways pages are a nuisance with many causes:

- **Phone scans.** Your phone guessed the orientation from how you held it, and guessed wrong.
- **Scanners and copiers.** A document placed the wrong way on the glass comes out upside down.
- **Landscape pages in portrait documents.** Spreadsheets and charts printed in landscape appear sideways inside a report.
- **Mixed bundles.** Merging several files can bring together pages with different orientations.

A reviewer, examiner or bank officer who has to tilt their head to read your documents is not off to a good start. Fixing orientation takes seconds.

## How to rotate PDF pages step by step

1. Open [Rotate PDF](/tools/rotate-pdf/) and choose your PDF.
2. Look at the page previews. Each page has its own small rotate buttons for quick fixes.
3. To rotate several pages together, click to select them, or use the selection buttons (All, None, Odd, Even).
4. Press **Left** (90° anticlockwise), **Right** (90° clockwise) or **180°**. With nothing selected, all pages turn.
5. Made a mess? Press **Reset rotation** to return the selected pages to their original orientation.
6. Download the rotated PDF.

## Which rotation do you need?

| What you see | Press |
|---|---|
| Text runs from bottom to top (page lying on its left side) | Right |
| Text runs from top to bottom (page lying on its right side) | Left |
| Page is upside down | 180° |
| Landscape chart in a portrait report, and you want it upright for screen reading | Left or Right, depending on the chart |

Remember that "Right" is the same as 90° clockwise, and two Rights make 180°.

## Rotating only some pages

The odd and even buttons exist for a very common problem. When a double-sided document is scanned on some machines, every back page comes out upside down. Select **Even**, press **180°**, and the whole document is fixed in one move.

For a report with a few landscape tables, click just those pages in the preview and rotate them. The rest of the document is unaffected.

## Viewer rotation vs saved rotation

| | Rotating in a PDF viewer | Rotating with Rotate PDF |
|---|---|---|
| Changes what you see now | Yes | Yes |
| Changes the file itself | Usually not | Yes |
| Others see it rotated | No | Yes |
| Portal previews show it upright | No | Yes |

If you plan to upload the file to a portal, email it, or print it, save the rotation.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- **Rotating every page when only one was wrong.** If any pages are selected, only those turn; if none are, all of them do. Check the selection before pressing a button.
- **Rotating in the viewer and assuming it saved.** Always download the rotated copy and reopen it to confirm.
- **Fixing orientation after merging many files.** It works, but it is easier to rotate each file before you merge, especially for long bundles.
- **Confusing rotation with cropping.** Rotation turns a page; it does not remove white margins. For that, use [Crop PDF](/tools/crop-pdf/).

## Rotation as part of a bigger clean-up

Rotation is often one step in tidying a scanned file:

1. Rotate sideways pages so everything reads upright.

## Example: a GST notice reply bundle

A small trader in Surat is replying to a notice and has to attach invoices, a ledger extract and bank statements. The ledger was printed in landscape and scanned, so those six pages are sideways, while the invoices were photographed upside down on a desk.

He opens the combined PDF, selects the six ledger pages and presses **Right**, then selects the invoice pages and presses **180°**. The download now reads upright from start to finish. The officer reviewing it does not have to rotate anything on screen.

## Does rotation affect printing?

Yes, in a good way. Printers follow the rotation saved in the file, so a correctly rotated PDF prints upright without you fiddling with print settings. Landscape pages that you leave in landscape will print sideways on a portrait sheet, which is usually what you want for wide tables. If you rotate them to portrait instead, the table will be printed across the narrow side of the paper and may become small, so think about whether the document is mainly for screen reading or for print.

## What about pages that are slightly tilted?

Rotate PDF turns pages in exact steps of 90 degrees. It cannot straighten a photo taken at a slight angle, where the text runs a few degrees off horizontal. For that kind of problem, the fix is at the scanning stage: use a scanner app that corrects perspective, such as Scan to PDF, and capture the page again. A crisp, straight page is far easier to read than a tilted one, and it also compresses better.

## Checklist before you download

- Every page reads upright when you scroll through the preview.
- Landscape tables are oriented the way you intend for screen or print.
- No page was accidentally turned twice.
- You still have the original file, in case you need to start again.

## Privacy and speed

Rotation is a light operation, so even long documents finish almost instantly. It runs entirely in your browser: the file is read from your device and the rotated copy is saved back to it. Nothing is uploaded, which matters when the pages are bank statements, invoices or identity documents.
